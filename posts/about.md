# My Synthetic Diary
I have trained a GPT2 model on my diary entries from 2017 to 2026. It now produces a fictional diary entry every couple of days, posted here without my interference.

I am not actually sure how to go about this - for now I made an RSS feed and will post to Mastodon too if the admins are ok with this. 


 You can fork [this template](https://github.com/academicpages/academicpages.github.io) right now, modify the configuration and Markdown files, add your own PDFs and other content, and have your own site for free, with no ads!
